# Version one scope: three scrutiny reports for the discovery test

Status: proposal for review, 29 October 2026. Once approved, this document is authoritative. Anything not listed under "In scope" is out of scope until Victor approves a change, and approved changes are recorded in section 10.

## 1. Objective

Produce **three scrutiny reports** on real construction bid packages, and deliver them to prospective owner or lender clients, to test one proposition:

> An independent, evidence-cited scrutiny of a construction bid finds material issues that the reader would otherwise have missed or found later, at a cost and speed the reader would pay for.

Version one is the smallest system that can produce those three reports to a professional standard. It is not a product release.

## 2. What a scrutiny report contains

One report per bid package. A package contains the tender documents and one or more submissions.

1. **Summary for decision-makers.** The five to ten most consequential findings, each with its basis label.
2. **Scope and method.** Documents received, checks run, checks not run and why, analyst review, and a disclosure that AI was used and how.
3. **Price integrity.** Arithmetic errors, omitted or altered items, provisional sums, zero-rated and "included" items, and their combined price effect.
4. **Qualifications and departures.** Every exclusion, assumption, qualification and departure from the conditions of contract, quoted and cited.
5. **Compliance.** Mandatory submission requirements, and the technical requirements chosen for the engagement, with status and evidence.
6. **Security and programme.** Bonds, guarantees and insurances against the requirements; completion and sectional dates against the contract.
7. **Rate review.** Outliers against peers (four or more bids) or against a reference estimate, where one is supplied.
8. **Clarification questions** for each bidder (owner profile), or a risk register (lender profile).
9. **Limitations.** What was not examined, unreadable pages, and assumptions made.
10. **Appendix.** Finding register with document, page and quote, plus the hash of the run record.

Each finding states its basis:

- **Fact.** A verified quote or a deterministic computation. Example: "Item 3.2.4: 40 m³ × S$180 = S$7,200; stated S$7,400."
- **Interpretation.** A judgement. Example: "The cap on liquidated damages at 5% shifts delay risk to the employer."

## 3. In scope

| # | Capability | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engagement set-up from `engagement.yaml` and `register.yaml` in `EVALIA_DATA_DIR` | Runs without any path to client data in code or the repository |
| 2 | Ingestion of text-layer PDF, XLSX and DOCX, with page-level provenance | Every page either yields text or appears in a "no text" list in the report's limitations |
| 3 | Requirement extraction from tender documents (model-assisted), curated by the analyst in a workbook | 100% threshold preservation on the curated set (deterministic gate) |
| 4 | Compliance resolution for mandatory submission requirements and for a selected set of technical requirements | Every status other than `not_addressed` or `not_applicable` carries at least one verified quote; every `not_addressed` states where the system looked |
| 5 | BQ checks BQ-01 to BQ-06 (see `02-architecture.md`, section 6.2) | 100% recall on synthetic packages with injected errors |
| 6 | Rate review (BQ-07) with two modes: peers when there are four or more bids, otherwise a reference estimate | Each mode labelled; when neither is possible, the report says so |
| 7 | Qualifications and departures register (QX-01, CON-01) | Every entry quoted and verified; the analyst confirms the register is complete for each submission |
| 8 | Security package (SEC-01) and programme dates (PRG-01) | Comparisons are deterministic once values are extracted and confirmed |
| 9 | Review workbook round trip: export proposed findings, import decisions | No finding reaches the report unless accepted or amended; rejections and amendments need a reason |
| 10 | DOCX and Markdown report from a template in the construction pack | Matches section 2; every finding carries a basis label and a citation |
| 11 | LLM gateway with residency enforcement, caching and provenance (`03-llm-layer.md`, section 12) | A call that breaks the engagement's policy is refused; reports regenerate from cache |
| 12 | Run record for each run | Lists hashes of all inputs and outputs, versions, model identifiers and cost |
| 13 | Synthetic golden set with injected gaps and errors | Generated by script; visibly fictitious; committed to the repository |
| 14 | One jurisdiction overlay, matching the discovery packages | Registry and clause rules checked against current official sources |

## 4. Out of scope for version one

Explicitly excluded, including everything the DeepSeek transcript designed for a procuring entity:

- Web UI, multi-user accounts, role-based access control, single sign-on.
- Two-envelope sealing, Merkle seals, hash-chained audit ledger, checkpoints, write-once export, offline verifier.
- Award computation (lowest compliant price, Quality-Fee Method, price-quality methods).
- HSM key custody, quorum unseal, FROST key generation, key rotation ceremonies.
- RFC 3161, transparency-log or Bitcoin anchoring.
- Criterion scoring with weights (SCR-01).
- OCR of scanned documents. If a discovery package needs it, the analyst records the affected pages as a limitation, or transcribes the key figures by hand and labels them as such.
- Parsing of Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project files, and analysis of drawings or CAD.
- Bids in languages other than English.
- PostgreSQL, vector databases and any server deployment.
- More than one jurisdiction overlay.

## 5. Workflow for one report

```
1  analyst creates engagement folder, engagement.yaml, register.yaml   (manual)
2  evalia ingest            → work/ text and tables, page provenance
3  evalia extract-reqs      → review/requirements.xlsx (model-assisted)
4  analyst curates requirements; threshold gate must pass             (manual)
5  evalia check             → BQ, security and programme findings      (deterministic)
6  evalia resolve           → compliance, qualifications, departures   (model-assisted)
7  evalia review-export     → review/findings.xlsx
8  analyst accepts, amends or rejects every finding                     (manual)
9  evalia review-import     → decisions recorded
10 evalia report            → reports/<package>.docx and .md, runs/<run>.json
11 second reader checks the report before release                       (manual)
```

The command names are illustrative. Step 11 is a human quality gate: a second qualified person reads the report against the documents before it leaves Evalia.

## 6. Quality gates for each report

A report is released only if all of these hold:

1. Every cited quote is verified on its cited page (automated).
2. Every threshold in the curated requirements matches its source sentence (automated).
3. Every finding in the report has review state `accepted` or `amended` (automated).
4. Every finding carries a basis label of Fact or Interpretation (automated).
5. The limitations section lists every page without text and every check not run (automated).
6. A second reader has signed off (manual; recorded in the run record).

## 7. What the discovery test measures

Proposed measures. The choice of measures is Victor's decision.

| Measure | How collected |
|---|---|
| Analyst hours per report, split by workflow step | Logged by the analyst |
| Findings proposed against findings accepted, by check | Run records and review workbooks |
| Findings the reader rates as new to them, and as material | Structured debrief with the reader |
| Price effect of arithmetic and omission findings, as a share of the bid | Report appendix |
| Model cost per report | Run records |
| Reader's stated willingness to pay, and at what price point | Debrief |
| Errors found in Evalia's report after release | Reader feedback; second-reader notes |

## 8. Milestones

Sequenced by exit criterion. Effort estimates are rough and should be revised after M1.

| Milestone | Exit criterion | Rough effort |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Scope approved | This document and `02-architecture.md` approved; open questions in section 9 answered | — |
| M1 Skeleton and synthetic golden set | Engine skeleton, construction pack v0 stub, synthetic packages with injected errors; CI runs tests on synthetic data only | 1 to 2 weeks |
| M2 Deterministic path | Ingestion, register, BQ-01 to BQ-06, SEC-01, PRG-01, review round trip, report template; 100% recall on injected BQ errors | 2 to 3 weeks |
| M3 Model-assisted path | Gateway (two adapters, residency, cache), requirement extraction with threshold gate, resolver, QX-01, CON-01; golden-set metrics recorded for at least two profiles | 2 to 3 weeks |
| M4 First real report | Report 1 produced on a real package, passed the quality gates, debriefed | 1 to 2 weeks |
| M5 Reports 2 and 3 | Reports 2 and 3 delivered, with fixes from report 1; discovery measures collected | 2 to 3 weeks |

**Speculation.** The total is roughly 8 to 13 weeks of one developer working with one construction analyst. The largest uncertainties are the condition of real bid documents (scans, spreadsheets saved as images) and the time it takes to obtain packages.

## 9. Open questions to settle before M1

1. **Who are the three readers?** Owners, lenders or a mix? This decides the report profiles and whether single-bid benchmarking is needed.
2. **Which packages, and on what terms?** Live or recent tenders? Is written consent in place for Evalia to process them, and for which model providers?
3. **Which jurisdiction?** This decides the one overlay to build.
4. **Will reference estimates or cost plans be supplied?**
5. **Who is the analyst, and who is the second reader?** The quality gates need two named people.
6. **Liability and positioning.** How should reports be worded to stay advisory, and is professional indemnity cover needed for the discovery test?
7. **Storage.** Do the clients accept OneDrive as the location of their documents?
