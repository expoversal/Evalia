You are screening clauses from a construction tender for bidder obligations.

A BIDDER OBLIGATION is a requirement the bidder/contractor must satisfy in their
submission or performance. It is signalled by: shall, must, is required to, is to,
will provide, submit, comply with, ensure that.

NOT a bidder obligation:
  - Employer/Engineer obligations ("The Employer shall provide site access")
  - Descriptions, definitions, recitals
  - Evaluation methodology ("Bids will be evaluated on...")
  - Boilerplate without a subject obligation
  - Cross-references alone ("See Clause 4.2")
