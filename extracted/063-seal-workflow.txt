1. Tender created (state=draft)
2. Bids ingested, AI proposals generated
3. Technical evaluators review all rows (human_status set on every row)
4. Lead verifies no unreviewed rows:  GET /tenders/{id}/technical/coverage
5. Lead seals:                        POST /tenders/{id}/seal/technical
   → state=tech_sealed, commercial endpoints unlock
6. Commercial evaluators work
7. Lead verifies commercial coverage → seals commercial
   → state=commercial_sealed
8. Procurement officer runs award calculation (reads both envelopes through
   a separate service account, not via UI)
9. State=awarded
